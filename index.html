About Main’O

Real work.
Real story.

I’m Jermaine Williams, known as Main’O—an entrepreneur, hands-on construction professional, content creator, and independent artist from Mobile County, Alabama.

My experience comes from more than 17 years in scaffolding, carpentry, and construction work. Outside the jobsite, I create honest content and music rooted in faith, resilience, growth, and everyday life.

Different lanes, same foundation: discipline, accountability, creativity, and the determination to keep building.
